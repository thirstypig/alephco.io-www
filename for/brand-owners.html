For Brand Owners

See the records your importer keeps for your products

When an importer brings your products into the US on Aleph, it can share them with you. You see each product’s compliance records, read-only. The importer keeps them up to date and chooses what you see.

How it works

Your importer shares, you accept

Sharing starts with your importer. Ask your importer to add you on Aleph.

1

Your importer adds you

On a product’s supply chain, the importer adds your company as Brand owner with the email you sign in with, and ticks the other products you own.

2

Aleph emails you

Already on Aleph? Sign in and open Network → Connections. New to Aleph? The email has a sign-up link with your address filled in. Sign up with that same address and confirm it.

3

You accept

An owner or manager at your company accepts or declines. Accepted products appear in your Products list, each marked Managed by your importer.

What you see

Your products’ records, read-only

The product page

The same product page the importer sees, read-only: product name, UPC, category, country of origin, and the supply chain from the factory to your company.

Record counts and requirements

How many required records are on file for each regulation, such as “3 of 8 recorded”, and whether each requirement is recorded. A count is not a rating.

Certificate status and PDF

The CPSC certificate’s stage, dates and the rules it certifies, and a download of the certificate PDF. The PDF names the factory, because CPSC requires the certificate to (16 CFR 1110.11(a)(5)).

Documents and listings

Each document’s type and dates, and a UL, ETL or CSA listing’s type and expiry. No file names or files.

Shared with you

Your Supply chain, Alerts and Readiness Dashboard pages each add a Shared with you table: the importer’s alerts due in the next 180 days, and each product’s record counts.

Not on your plan

Shared products belong to the importer. They do not count toward your plan’s products, and they stay out of your own downloads.

The importer stays in control

Each importer chooses what you see

  • Compliance counts and alerts are on unless the importer turns them off for you.
  • Factory and exporter names are off unless the importer turns them on. Off, they read Managed by the importer. On, you see company names only, never contacts or addresses.
  • You never see the importer’s other buyers, its other products, or its document files.
  • You cannot add, edit, upload, attest or send anything. The importer manages the product.
  • The importer sees each time you open a product or download a certificate, and can stop sharing at any time.
  • Aleph does not file with CBP or CPSC for anyone.
For importers

Share products with the brand that owns them

Open a product, then Supply chain → Add Partner, and choose the role Brand owner. Type the brand’s name and the email it signs in with. The brand sees nothing until it accepts, and you can stop at any time. Sharing with your customs broker.

Did your importer invite you?

Log in, or use the sign-up link in the invitation email, then accept under Network → Connections.
