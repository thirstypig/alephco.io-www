The Challenge

Children’s products face the toughest rules

CPC is mandatory

Children’s products subject to a children’s product safety rule must be certified in a Children’s Product Certificate based on third-party testing. An imported product not accompanied by a required certificate shall be refused admission (15 U.S.C. 2066(a)).

Periodic testing required

Manufacturers and importers of children’s products must keep testing periodically — at least once a year unless a longer interval under 16 CFR 1107.21(c) or (d) applies — even if nothing about the product changed.

300+ recalls a year

CPSC’s recall database lists more than 300 recalls in each of 2023, 2024, and 2025. Knowing violations of the Consumer Product Safety Act can carry civil penalties of up to $100,000 each before inflation adjustments (15 U.S.C. 2069).

The Solution

CPCs and lab reports, organized by product

Aleph keeps the certificates you issue and the lab reports behind them on file against each product, with the requirements cited. You issue and file the CPC; Aleph does not generate or file it for you.

Upload lab reports

Upload test reports from your CPSC-accepted lab and link each one to the products it covers. Aleph reads the document and suggests its dates for you to confirm.

Keep the CPC on file

Upload the CPC you issued to the product. Under 16 CFR 1110.11, a certificate must contain: product identification, each rule certified to, the certifier, the records custodian, date and place of manufacture, date and place of testing, and the attestation.

Track expiration dates

Give a lab report or certificate an expiration date and Aleph emails you a digest before it comes due, so a periodic test doesn’t slip.

Features

Built for children’s product importers

Tracking label requirements, cited

Children’s products need permanent distinguishing marks on the product and its packaging, to the extent practicable (15 U.S.C. 2063(a)(5)). Aleph lists the requirement with its citation; the marks themselves are applied by you and your factory.

Every requirement cited

Each requirement on a product’s checklist names the rule it comes from. Which children’s product safety rules your product is certified to is your decision, made with your lab.

Testing-lab directory

Browse a directory of testing labs suggested by product category and the country where your products are made. Confirming that a lab is CPSC-accepted for your tests stays your call.

Multi-SKU dashboard

See which products across your children’s catalog have their documents on file and which requirements are still missing.

Compliance package downloads

Download a product’s compliance package — its certificates and lab reports in one file — to share with retailers or customs brokers.

Audit-ready records

Every CPC and lab report is stored against its product, and changes are logged in the product’s history.
