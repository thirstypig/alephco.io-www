For Customs Brokers

Your clients’ filing IDs, one sheet per shipment

A free, read-only Aleph account for licensed customs brokers. Your importer clients choose which products you see. You download the FDA food and CPSC certificate IDs your filing needs, as CSV or PDF, without chasing them by email.

How it starts

By invitation, then a check, then your clients

Broker accounts are by invitation from Aleph. There is no public broker sign-up.

1

Aleph invites you

Ask us for an account, or a client names you and Aleph invites you. Sign up with the address the invitation was sent to.

2

Aleph checks your brokerage

Aleph finds your brokerage on CBP’s Permitted Customs Brokers Listing, then confirms you by your work email’s domain or by calling the phone number CBP lists. Until then, your account shows no client data.

3

Clients share with your brokerage

A client invites you, or you ask a client by email from your home page. Nothing about a client shows, not even its name, until it agrees and picks the products you see. Colleagues Aleph has checked at your brokerage see the same clients.

What you get

The IDs your filing needs, in one place

A broker sheet for each product

For each shared product: the FDA food details (importer name, email and D-U-N-S number, the manufacturer and its FDA food facility registration, country of origin, FDA product code) and the CPSC certificate IDs (Certifier ID, Product ID, Version ID). A blank reads Not provided. Download it as CSV or PDF.

One sheet per shipment

Tick the products on a shipment and add your own reference, such as a PO, invoice or entry number. You get one PDF with a page per product, or one CSV with the same columns as the single-product sheet, so an import you mapped once keeps working.

Always the current data

Recent shipments are saved for your whole brokerage and download again at any time. Each download is built from the client’s current records, and only from products it still shares.

A daily change email

When a shared product’s CPSC Version ID, CPSC’s status for the certificate, the importer’s D-U-N-S number or the manufacturer’s FDA food facility registration changes, you get an email naming the product and the ID. Open Aleph to read the new value. Unsubscribe in one click.

Hold documents on request

When CPSC or FDA holds an entry, ask the client for the hold documents with the entry number. The client chooses the files and sends them from Aleph, and you get a link to download them.

Supply chain and record counts

For each shared product, its manufacturer and exporter (name and country) and how many of its required records are on file, such as “3 of 8 recorded”. A count is not a rating. The client can turn either off.

The client stays in control

Read-only, and visible to your client

  • Your client chooses the products you see: all of them, or the ones it ticks.
  • Your client sees an access log of each time your brokerage opens its products or downloads a sheet, and each change email Aleph sends you.
  • Your client can stop sharing at any time. Access ends immediately, and you get an email.
  • You cannot upload, edit or open a client’s documents in Aleph. A document reaches you only when the client sends it.
  • Your account is free and never takes a seat on your client’s plan.
What Aleph does not do

You file the entry

  • Aleph does not file with CBP, CPSC or FDA for anyone. You and your client decide what to file.
  • The broker sheet repeats what your client entered. Aleph does not check the IDs with FDA, CPSC or CBP.
Bringing a client to Aleph

An invite code for your clients

Ask us for an invite code to give your clients. A client that signs up with it gets 20% off its first year on an annual plan. Monthly plans get no discount, and Aleph pays no referral fee.
