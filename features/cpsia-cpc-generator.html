The Problem

Manual CPC creation is broken

Error-prone data entry

A CPC must carry seven elements under 16 CFR 1110.11(a), including each rule you certify to, where and when the product was made and tested, and the CPSC-accepted lab. One wrong entry and the certificate is wrong.

Hours per certificate

Manually cross-referencing lab reports, looking up regulation citations, and formatting PDFs takes hours per product — time you don’t have.

Missed re-test deadlines

CPSIA requires periodic third-party testing — at least once a year, or every two or three years under a production testing plan or ISO/IEC 17025 lab testing (16 CFR 1107.21). Without tracking, re-test dates slip by.

The Solution

Your CPC records, organized and cited

Aleph keeps your certificates and lab reports with each children’s product, lists the records CPSIA requires you to keep, and helps you find a lab. Whether a product complies is your call, and so is the certificate.

Upload certificates and lab reports

Upload your CPC and the test reports from your CPSC-accepted lab. Aleph’s AI suggests each document’s type and dates for you to confirm, then files it under the product.

A cited records checklist

Each product’s checklist lists the records 16 CFR 1107.26 requires you to keep — the certificate, third-party test records, periodic testing, and more — each with its citation, so you can check it against the rule yourself.

Track & maintain

Give lab reports and certificates an expiration or re-test date, and Aleph alerts you before it passes — in the app and by email.

Features

Everything in one place for your CPC records

Lab directory with smart scoring

Browse a directory of 10 testing labs — 7 of them listed as CPSC-accepted — with scoring that ranks by product type match and presence in your manufacturer’s country. Confirm a lab’s current acceptance for your tests on CPSC’s own list.

Lab report & certificate upload

Upload your lab test reports and certificates. Aleph’s AI reads each one and suggests its type and dates, and you confirm — less retyping of what’s already on the page.

The seven CPC elements

A CPC must carry seven elements under 16 CFR 1110.11(a): the product, each rule it is certified to, the certifier, the records contact, the date and place of manufacture, the date and place of testing (with the lab), and the certifier’s attestation. Since July 8, 2026, imported products’ certificate data must be eFiled at entry (16 CFR 1110.13).

Find a lab from the CPSIA page

The Find a lab button on the CPSIA page opens the lab directory narrowed to the countries your products are made in.

Expiration alerts

CPSIA requires periodic testing. Set an expiration date on a lab report or certificate and Aleph warns you ahead of it — 30 days by default, or 7 to 90 days if you prefer.

Standards by product type

The lab finder lists the standards typically tested for your product type — such as lead in paint (16 CFR 1303) and phthalates (16 CFR 1307) — to rank labs. Which rules apply to your product is still yours to decide.

Multi-SKU management

See every product that falls under CPSIA in one list, and open any product’s documents and checklist to find what needs attention.

Records on hand

Certificates and lab reports stay with the product, and your uploads and changes are recorded in your action log — so you can produce records when CPSC asks.
