PFAS Documents

PFAS documents, kept product by product

PFAS laws vary by state and product category. Aleph does not have a PFAS checklist and does not decide which state laws apply to a product. What it does: keep the PFAS test reports and supplier statements you collect on the product they cover, read their dates for you, and alert you before one expires.

The Problem

PFAS regulation is a moving target

State by state, different laws

Maine, Minnesota, California — each state has its own PFAS rules, with different product categories, triggers, and dates. On top of those, EPA’s TSCA PFAS reporting rule (40 CFR part 705) reaches manufacturers and importers, including of articles.

Invisible chemicals

State PFAS laws name everyday categories — Minnesota’s bans cover cookware, carpets, cosmetics, and juvenile products; California’s covers textile articles. Your products may contain PFAS from manufacturing processes you don’t directly control.

Rules keep changing

PFAS rules are still being written and amended, at the state and federal level. Keeping up manually is a full-time job.

The Solution

One place for your PFAS record

Aleph does not tell you which state PFAS laws apply to a product — that call stays yours. It keeps the evidence you gather in one place, on the product it belongs to.

Filed on the product

Upload a PFAS document once and file it against every product it covers, so each product’s record shows what you have.

Supplier statements on file

When a supplier sends you a PFAS statement, upload it against the product it covers. It sits next to the test reports, with its date and expiry.

Test reports on file

Upload lab test reports against each product. Aleph reads the document and suggests its dates for you to confirm. Reading the results against each state’s rules stays your call.

Features

Your PFAS evidence, organized

Expiry alerts

Give a test report or supplier statement an expiry date and Aleph alerts you before it lapses, so you can ask for a fresh one in time.

Upload once, read for you

Aleph reads each uploaded document and suggests its key dates, so you confirm rather than retype them.

Which laws apply stays your call

Aleph does not map state PFAS laws to your products or tell you whether a product complies. It keeps the evidence organized so you, or your advisor, can make that call.

In the Compliance Package

A product’s Compliance Package ZIP gathers the documents on file for it, so you can hand them over in one download.

Documents in one record

Test reports and supplier statements are stored on the product they cover, with dates. Show what you have when regulators or retailers ask.

Materials checked against a PFAS list

Each confirmed material or ingredient is checked against Aleph’s PFAS list by exact CAS number or name. A match shows a notice when PFAS is switched on for the product, or a suggestion to switch it on when it is off. The list is a selection of PFAS, not every PFAS, and a match does not say whether a state rule applies.
