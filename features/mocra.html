MoCRA Module

MoCRA compliance for cosmetics brands — without the spreadsheet

The Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act put new, ongoing obligations on every facility and product. Aleph tracks facility registration renewals, product listing updates, and safety substantiation status in one place — alongside Prop 65 and PFAS for the same catalog.

The Problem

MoCRA didn’t just add a form — it added a calendar

Registration expires on a timer

Facility registration renews every two years, product listings update annually. Miss either and you’re out of compliance with no product change at all — just a date that passed.

Safety substantiation, per product

Every product needs adequate substantiation of safety before it ships — a safety assessment, and often a Cosmetic Product Safety Report. Tracking which products have it, and which don’t, by hand doesn’t scale past a handful of SKUs.

Not the only regulation on your label

Cosmetics sold in the US often carry Prop 65 exposure and, increasingly, PFAS scrutiny too. Tracking MoCRA in one tool and everything else in another means nothing talks to anything.

The Solution

One record per product, every regulation it touches

Facility registration, product listing, and safety substantiation status live next to Prop 65 and PFAS for the same product — not in three separate systems.

Facility registration tracking

Record your facility registration and let Aleph track the two-year renewal window — with an alert before it lapses, not after.

Product listing status

Track each product’s listing and its annual update date, plus who’s named as the Responsible Person and label contact — both required fields, both easy to lose track of.

Safety substantiation status

Upload the safety assessment, Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR), toxicology reports, and lab results Aleph reads and files under the product — and shows you exactly what’s missing.

Features

Cosmetics compliance, in the same platform as everything else

Biennial + annual renewal alerts

Facility registration renews every two years, product listings every year — different clocks, tracked automatically so neither one runs out silently.

Responsible Person & label contact

Every listed product needs a named Responsible Person and label contact. Aleph flags any product missing either before it becomes a compliance gap.

Document-driven, not form-driven

Upload your safety assessment, CPSR, tox reports, or lab results — Aleph reads and organizes them automatically. No manual data entry for what’s already on the document.

Prop 65 & PFAS on the same product

Cosmetics carry more than one exposure. Track MoCRA substantiation, Prop 65 warnings, and PFAS disclosure for the same SKU, side by side.

Audit-ready package

Generate a substantiation package for a product on request — every document, every date, in one export.

Included in every plan

MoCRA is a compliance module like FSVP or CPSIA — no separate fee, no add-on. It’s included the moment you flag a product as a cosmetic.
