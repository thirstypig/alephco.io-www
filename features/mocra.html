What the rule asks

MoCRA added a calendar, not just a form

The Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act put ongoing obligations on cosmetics facilities, products and the responsible person.

Registration and listing

Facility registration renews every two years, and product listings are updated annually (21 U.S.C. 364c).

Safety substantiation

The responsible person must ensure, and keep records supporting, adequate substantiation of safety for every product (21 U.S.C. 364d(a)).

Label contact

A product listing names the responsible person, and a cosmetic label must carry contact information for adverse event reports.

What you still do yourself

You file with FDA; Aleph does not

  • Register your facility and list your products with FDA yourself, through Cosmetics Direct.
  • Decide whether a product’s safety is adequately substantiated, with your safety assessor.
  • Submit records to FDA when it asks. Aleph does not submit anything.
