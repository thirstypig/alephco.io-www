What Aleph does

One sentence per regulation

Each links to the regulation’s page: what the rule asks, what Aleph does, what you still do yourself, and plan availability.

FDA · 21 CFR 1 Subpart L

FSVP

Record your foreign supplier evaluations, hazard analyses and verification activities as signed FSVP records, and download them as one inspection package when FDA asks.

USDA APHIS · 9 CFR 94

USDA dairy

For imported milk products such as ice cream: answer where the milk came from, then keep the APHIS import permit, the government vet certificate or the organic import certificate on the product.

CPSC · 16 CFR 1110

CPSIA / CPC and GCC

Build the CPC or GCC from your product’s records for you to attest, print it as a PDF, and keep it and its lab reports on file against the product.

CA · 27 CCR 25600

Prop 65

Search OEHHA’s chemical list, record your own warning decision for each product, and generate short-form or long-form warning labels as PDFs.

States · ME, MN, NY + more

PFAS

There is no PFAS checklist in Aleph: keep your PFAS test reports and supplier statements on the products they cover, with an alert before one expires.

EU · Reg. (EC) 1907/2006

REACH (EU)

For articles sold into the EU: the Article 33 and Article 7(2) duties for a Candidate List substance above 0.1% weight by weight, each row cited and naming whose duty it is.

FDA · FD&C Act ch. VI

MoCRA

Track cosmetics facility registration renewals, what each product listing needs and each product’s safety substantiation records. You file with FDA; Aleph does not.

Not a law

UL listings

Keep a product’s UL, ETL or CSA listing certificate, its file number, expiry date and test report. A listing is not a law Aleph checks.

Every product’s confirmed ingredients or materials are checked against California’s Prop 65 list, the EU Candidate List (REACH) and Aleph’s PFAS list, by exact CAS number or name. A match means the chemical is on the list, not how much of it is in the product — whether anything is required stays your call. How requirements are cited.

UL listings · Not a law

UL, ETL and CSA listings

Keep a product’s UL, ETL or CSA listing certificate, its file number, expiry date and test report. A listing is not a law Aleph checks.

What a listing is

A UL, ETL or CSA listing is a certification you chose, or one a buyer or retailer requires. It is not a law Aleph checks, so it carries no cited requirement and is not counted on the checklist. UL listings on Learn.

What Aleph does

Keeps the listing certificate on the product, with its file number and expiry date and the test report behind it, and reminds you before the certificate expires.

What you still do yourself

Get and renew the listing with your certifier, and keep the product as listed. On every plan, Free included.
