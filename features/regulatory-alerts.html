Regulatory Alerts

Federal rule changes, tracked — so you don’t have to check

CPSIA and FSVP requirements change through the same rulemaking process as everything else at 16 CFR and 21 CFR. Aleph checks the federal register every week and emails you when a rule you’re watching changes — no manual monitoring, no missed amendment.

The Problem

Nobody tells you when the rule under your product changes

Amendments don’t announce themselves

A section of 16 CFR Part 1110 or 21 CFR Part 1 can be amended without any notice landing in your inbox. The certificate or process you built around the old wording is the thing that goes stale.

Checking by hand doesn’t scale

The federal register is public, but reading it every week for the two or three parts that actually touch your catalog isn’t a job anyone has time for on top of running an import business.

You find out at the worst time

The usual way a compliance team learns a rule changed is when a certificate gets rejected or an inspector asks about the old version — after the fact, not before.

The Solution

Pick what you watch. Get an email when it changes.

Choose which regulations to watch on your account, and Aleph checks the federal register for you every week.

Watch what applies to you

Turn on alerts for CPSIA and FSVP from your account settings. Only the regulations you actually watch ever generate an email.

Checked weekly, automatically

Aleph queries the federal register’s public data every week for the CPSIA and FSVP sections your account is set up to track — no manual lookup required.

An email, with the citation

When a section you’re watching changes, you get an email naming the exact rule and a link to the full text — not just a vague notice that “something changed.”

What’s Actually Tracked Today

Federal rules, checked weekly — here’s exactly what that covers

CPSIA certificates — 16 CFR Part 1110

The Certificates of Compliance requirements children’s-product importers file under, tracked at the section level.

FSVP — 21 CFR Part 1

The Foreign Supplier Verification Program requirements food importers work under, tracked at the section level.

A weekly check, not real-time

Aleph checks once a week. If a rule changes the same day you look, you’ll hear about it on the next check — not instantly.

A page showing everything matched

Every change your account’s watched regulations have matched lives on one page in your account, not just in your inbox.

Not yet covering state law

Prop 65 and PFAS obligations are set at the state level, not through the federal register — this alert doesn’t track those yet. Aleph’s Prop 65 module separately keeps its own chemical list current.

Included, no separate fee

Regulatory alerts are a feature of your account, not a paid add-on — turn on what you want to watch.
