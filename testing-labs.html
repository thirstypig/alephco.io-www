Testing lab directory

Every lab on CPSC’s list of accepted testing laboratories, plus the labs on Aleph’s own list. Search by lab name, CPSC Lab ID or city. A lab is accepted only for the scopes listed under it.

CPSC list as of …. Check a lab’s current status on CPSC’s lab search before you rely on it.

    About this list

    • The labs and their scopes come from CPSC’s public lab search, copied by Aleph by hand. CPSC publishes no download feed, so the copy is dated — the date is shown above.
    • Each lab shows its type as CPSC lists it (independent, firewalled or governmental) and the scopes CPSC has accepted it for. Check that the scopes cover each rule your product is tested to.
    • “Group” is Aleph’s match on the start of the lab’s name (for example, SGS). It does not say who owns the lab.
    • A lab marked “Not on CPSC’s accepted-lab list” is on Aleph’s own list only. It is not CPSC-accepted.
    • Aleph does not test products, and does not say a lab is right for your product. Choosing a lab stays your call.
