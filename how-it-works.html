How It Works

Four steps to organized compliance records

No compliance background required. Aleph shows you what each requirement asks for and where it comes from.

1

Tell us what you import

Create your account, add your products, and give each one a product type. Aleph matches product types to the compliance modules it covers, and you choose which modules to turn on.

2

See every requirement, cited

Each product gets a checklist of the requirements for the modules you turned on — FSVP, CPSIA, Prop 65, PFAS, MoCRA — each naming the regulation or statute behind it, so you can check it yourself.

3

Collect the evidence

Upload certificates and lab reports against the products they cover, record FSVP evaluations and Prop 65 warning decisions, and email suppliers a no-login link to fill in a PFAS declaration. Each checklist row shows what is still missing; whether a product complies stays your call.

4

Keep track of deadlines

Aleph emails you a digest when documents you’ve given an expiration date are coming due, and shows upcoming deadlines on your dashboard.
